## Detailed description
Save YouTube videos, get AI summaries, and search your knowledge base.

You watch hours of YouTube every week. How much of it do you actually remember?

BrainTube turns what you watch on YouTube and read on the web into a knowledge base any AI can use.

🔍 The problem
You watch a great video and think "I need to remember this." A week later it's gone.

Notes are scattered across apps, bookmarks never get reopened, and there is no way to search what someone said in a video you watched three months ago.

Sound familiar?

🧠 The fix
BrainTube saves YouTube videos, articles, LinkedIn posts and web pages to your own knowledge library, ready to search and use right away.

A clean side panel opens right next to what you're watching. No tab switching, no friction.

✨ What you can do
🧠 AI summaries: get notes, key takeaways and a structured outline from any YouTube video or web page. Remember it without rewatching.

💬 Chat with content: ask anything about a video or article. Answers come from the actual transcript, with timestamp sources that jump to the right moment.

📝 Highlights and notes: select transcript text to highlight it and leave timestamped notes linked to the exact moment.

🔍 Search your whole library: full-text search across every transcript, highlight and note you've saved. Find that one sentence in seconds.

⚡ One-click save: press Save in the side panel and the page or video you're on goes into your library for summaries, search and review.

📂 Collections and tags: organise knowledge by topic, project or interest.

🎯 Made for
YouTube learners who watch tutorials, lectures and deep dives
Researchers who need to find things again in hours of content
Students building a study library from video lectures
Professionals keeping up with webinars, podcasts and industry talks
Anyone who watches to learn, not just to scroll

⚡ How it works
Install BrainTube (takes 10 seconds)
Browse YouTube or any website
Click the 🧠 icon to open the side panel next to the content
Press Save and the AI writes a summary, transcript and key takeaways
Highlight, take notes, search and chat, all in the side panel
No new tabs, no copy-paste, no switching screens.

🔒 What BrainTube reads and where it goes
Saving pages and videos: only when you click Save, Save URL or Bookmark, BrainTube sends that tab's address and visible text to the BrainTube server, which reads and summarises it.
Saving AI conversations (claude.ai, chatgpt.com): only when you click Save Conversation in the BrainTube popup, BrainTube reads that conversation and sends its text to the BrainTube server, where Anthropic (Claude) summarises it. Nothing is captured automatically.
YouTube playlist sync: while you are signed in, adding a video to one of your own YouTube playlists on youtube.com sends that playlist's ID to BrainTube, which imports the playlist into your library. This happens in the background.
AI processing: summaries, chat and search use AI providers that process content on BrainTube's behalf: Anthropic, OpenAI and Google (Gemini).
No browsing history: apart from the cases above, BrainTube does not record or send the pages you visit.
No ads, and we don't sell your data. What you save is stored in your account only.
Your sign-in stays on this computer until you sign out, and web pages cannot read it.
Privacy policy: https://brain-tube.com/privacy
